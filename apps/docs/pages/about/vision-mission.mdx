# Vision & Mission

## Vision

**Money should be private the way cash is private.** When you hand someone a
banknote, no ledger records who paid whom, how much you hold, or where the note
came from. Public blockchains threw that away — every balance and every transfer
is permanently visible to anyone.

BlackVault's vision is a world where **on-chain finance is private by default**
without giving up self-custody, without a trusted mixer, and without asking
anyone's permission — with the option to prove and disclose when *you* choose.

## Mission

Make privacy the **default experience** of an everyday wallet, not an expert
tool bolted on afterwards:

1. **Invisible balances** — what you hold is yours to reveal.
2. **Unlinkable transfers** — no observer can connect sender to receiver.
3. **No metadata trail** — not on-chain, not through RPC providers, not through
   any third party that could tie your IP to your address.
4. **Real ownership** — your keys, exportable anytime; BlackVault can never move
   or freeze your funds.
5. **Auditable by choice** — privacy that you can selectively lift for proof or
   compliance, never a black box.

## Principles

| Principle | What it means in practice |
| --- | --- |
| **Private by default** | Every user gets privacy without configuring anything |
| **Self-custody always** | Every feature ships with an exit door and your keys in hand |
| **No new trust** | Standard contracts (ERC-5564/6538), no custodial mixer |
| **Honest about limits** | We document what is *not* hidden, clearly |
| **Ship safely** | Correctness and fund-safety over speed |
