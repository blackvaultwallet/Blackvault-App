# Getting Started

## 1. Create your vault

1. Open **[app.blackvault.cash](https://app.blackvault.cash)**.
2. Tap **Get Started** and sign in with **Google** or **email** — an embedded
   wallet is created for you instantly. No seed phrase to write down.
3. Your wallet address appears under **Receive**. The same address works on any
   EVM network, but BlackVault operates on **Robinhood Chain**.

> You always stay in control: the private key is exportable at any time from
> **Settings → Export private key**.

## 2. Fund it

Assets must live **on Robinhood Chain**. Two easy routes:

- **Bridge** — use [bridge.arbitrum.io](https://bridge.arbitrum.io) (the
  canonical route) or [relay.link](https://relay.link/bridge/robinhood) for
  small amounts. Send to your BlackVault address.
- **Swap on-chain** — Uniswap supports Robinhood Chain; swap ETH ↔ USDG there.

Keep a little **ETH for network fees** — fees on Robinhood Chain cost a
fraction of a cent, but a wallet with zero ETH can't move anything. The app
warns you when gas is empty.

## 3. Lock it (recommended)

**Settings → Biometric / Face ID → Enable.** From then on the app asks for
your face or fingerprint on every open, when entering the Private Vault, and
before exporting your key.

## 4. Claim your name

**Settings → Username → Get your name.** Check availability, confirm, and
`you.blackvaultwallet.eth` is yours — people can now pay a name instead of an
address.

## 5. Go private

Switch to the **Private Vault** tab and enable your private balance — one
signature derives your stealth identity. Share your `st:eth:…` address to
receive privately; see [Private Transfers](/guides/private-transfers).
