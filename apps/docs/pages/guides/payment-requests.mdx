# Payment Requests

Ask to get paid — privately.

## Create a request

1. **Private Vault → Payment** (the card glyph).
2. Pick the asset, an optional amount, and an optional note.
3. A **QR + link** is generated pointing at your **stealth address** — copy the
   link or let the payer scan the QR.

## What the payer sees

Opening the link (or scanning the QR) drops the payer into BlackVault with the
send form **prefilled** — recipient, amount, token. They slide to confirm and
the payment lands on a one-time address only you can spend.

- The payer never learns your balance or payment history.
- Observers can't connect the payment to your identity.
- Requests are recorded in your **Activity** as "Created payment request"
  (kept on this device only).

## Public requests

For a plain public payment, share your address or name from **Receive**
instead — payment requests are deliberately the *private* path.
