# Activity

Open the bell icon to see your portfolio chart and full transaction story.

## What appears

Activity merges three sources into one feed:

- **On-chain transfers** — token movements in and out of your wallet.
- **Private log** — your stealth sends and claims (`◈`), kept **on this device
  only**; nothing about them is exposed.
- **Wallet actions** — semantic entries recorded the moment you act: bought or
  changed a name (`✦`), paid via QR (`▣`), paid or created a payment request
  (`▤`). Where an action has a transaction, it replaces the raw transfer with
  a labeled entry.

The feed shows the last 24 hours, up to 20 rows — **See all** links to the
explorer for the complete on-chain history.

## Detail sheet

Tap any row: status, type (public / private / wallet action), network, token,
counterparty, time, and — for on-chain rows — the tx hash with a **View on
explorer** button. Private entries show *"unlinkable on-chain"* instead: there
is nothing on the explorer that connects them to you.

## Portfolio chart

The chart at the top tracks your total value (ETH holdings × live price
history + stables) across 1D / 1W / 1M / 1Y, with a draggable crosshair.
