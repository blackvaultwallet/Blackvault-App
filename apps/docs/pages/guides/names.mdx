# BlackVault Names

A name people can pay instead of a `0x` address:

```
you.blackvaultwallet.eth
```

Names are ENS subnames issued by BlackVault's own name service — resolvable
in-app today and, once the mainnet resolver is activated, anywhere ENS works
(other wallets included).

## Claim a name

1. **Settings → Username → Get your name.**
2. Type a label (3–20 lowercase letters, numbers, or dashes) and
   **Check availability**.
3. Review the purchase: the full name, the wallet it connects to, the price
   (free or a one-time USDG fee, shown up front), and the network.
4. **Slide to claim** — a live progress log runs, then the success check.

When claims are priced, payment is a USDG transfer verified **on-chain** by
the service before the name is recorded — and a payment can never be reused.

## Change your name

**Settings → Username → Change.** Same flow; your old name is released and the
new one takes its place. When claims are priced, each change costs the fee
again.

## Use a name

- **Send to a name** — type it in the Send field; the review screen shows the
  resolved address before anything signs.
- **Receive with your name** — it appears under your QR on every receive
  surface, tap-to-copy.
- One name per wallet; names always point at the wallet that claimed them.

> Private transfers still need your `st:eth:…` address — a name resolves to
> your **public** address and can't receive privately.
