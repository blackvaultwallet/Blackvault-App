# Send & Receive (public)

Public transfers are ordinary on-chain transactions — fast, cheap, and visible
on the explorer like any wallet.

## Send

1. Home → **Send**.
2. Enter the recipient: a `0x…` address **or a name** (`alice.blackvaultwallet.eth`
   or any ENS name).
3. Type the amount on the keypad and pick the asset (ETH or USDG).
4. Tap **Review send** — the review screen shows the amount, the name you
   typed **and the address it resolves to**, the network, and the fee note.
5. **Slide to send.** Nothing is ever signed before this slide.

> The app refuses early if your ETH can't cover the network fee, and it won't
> let a native-ETH send sweep your entire balance — the fee must stay payable.

## Receive

Home → **Receive** opens your address as a **QR code** with tap-to-copy, the
network selector (Robinhood Chain live; Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Optimism
coming), and your subname if you own one.

Anyone can send to that address from any wallet on Robinhood Chain.

## Scan to pay

The scan button (top-left) opens the camera. Point it at a BlackVault payment
QR or a plain address QR — the Send sheet opens prefilled. Works on iPhone and
Android.
