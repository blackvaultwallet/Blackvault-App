# Biometric Lock

Bank-style app lock backed by your device's biometrics — Face ID, fingerprint,
or Windows Hello (WebAuthn platform authenticator).

## Enable

**Settings → Biometric / Face ID → Enable** → your device prompts for
biometrics → the lock is armed.

## What it gates

| Moment | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| Opening the app (every page load / refresh) | Full-screen **Vault locked** — unlock with your face |
| First entry to the **Private Vault** per session | Verify again (cancellable) |
| **Export private key** | Verify before the key is shown |
| **Disabling the lock** | Verify — nobody can switch it off silently |

## What it is (and isn't)

- The lock protects the **app UI** on your device — someone holding your
  unlocked phone still can't open the vault, see private balances, or export
  the key.
- Sign-in stays **Google/email**, so switching accounts is unaffected.
- Custody itself is held by your wallet session and passkeys; the lock is an
  extra gate in front of it, not a replacement.

Disable any time from the same Settings row (biometric check required).
